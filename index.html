Billing & stock app · web + Android

Merlion: billing, menu & inventory

A single app for running the stall: sell, bill, cost recipes, track stock and expenses. It runs in any browser and as an Android app.

  • Fast billing with a UPI QR for the exact amount, cash with change, and cash on delivery.
  • Recipes per menu item show the cost per serving, profit and margin, and how many can be made from stock.
  • Each sale takes ingredients out of stock, earliest expiry first. Batches with expiry dates, expenses, and daily profit / cash in hand.
  • An Orders tab for website and WhatsApp pre-orders. Works offline and syncs between devices.
